Skywatch

Satellite passes, Starlink trains, meteor showers and moon phases for your location, filtered by darkness, cloud and light pollution.

Location

Tip: in Google Maps, long-press a spot and copy the coordinates shown, then paste them above.

Visibility filters

SunlightSky dark (sun below −6°) and satellite lit by the sun
Cloud coverHide satellite passes when cloud is above your limit
Light pollutionHide objects fainter than your sky allows

Cloud: hourly forecast is fetched from Open-Meteo (up to 16 days) when this page is allowed to reach the internet; otherwise your manual value is used. Light pollution can’t be looked up automatically: check your spot on lightpollutionmap.info and pick the matching Bortle class.

Events

Planet and eclipse events are calculated for your location. A 7x50 binocular shows about 7° of sky, so pairs closer than 3.5° fit in one view.

A train is 4+ Starlink satellites from the same launch (same orbit plane) following each other closely across the sky. Lower the spacing for tighter trains. Timing is accurate to about one calculation step (20–30 s).

Satellite passes are calculated for up to 7 days from the start date, because orbit data ages quickly. Meteor showers and moon phases cover the whole range, up to a year.

Satellite orbit data (TLE)

Paste current TLEs so passes are accurate. Get them from CelesTrak: bright satellites and Starlink (open, select all, copy). Data older than ~2 weeks gives wrong times.

Enter a location, paste TLEs for satellite passes, then choose Find events.
